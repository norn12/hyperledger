1) Do you need a blockchain in healthcare data sharing? A tertiary review
a review article which have a survey of why use blockchain based system insted of traditional healthcare data sharing system

2) Privacy preservation in blockchain-based healthcare data sharing: A systematic review 
Thes paper lead me to the conclusion that why not use public blockchain and use private or consortium blockchain system along with what privacy features are to consider while keeping various policies in mind; paper also covers various permissioned blockchain system survey and it implicitly says "Hyperledger Fabric is particularly well-suited for healthcare consortia because of its modular architecture and support for permissioned networks."

3) The Hyperledger Fabric as a Blockchain framework preserves the security of electronic health records  
It is a systematic literature review of 57 studies on Hyperledger Fabric in healthcare. Its main purpose is to find out  the role and applications of Fabric in securing electronic health records,it ensures fabric does provide integrity,privacy,security,availability.

4)A Hyperledger Fabric-Based System Framework for Healthcare Data Management
This paper shows that Hyperledger Fabric can act as a permissioned healthcare data-management layer, while actual medical data is stored off-chain. It uses identity management, access control, smart contracts, channels, and transaction records
